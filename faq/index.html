    • Gameplay

    What kind of game is Cloudheim?
    Cloudheim is a co-op fantasy “actioncraft” game set in a world post-Ragnarok. Explore new lands, battle enemies, and return to your flying base on Odin Shell to craft gear and prepare for your next journey. You can solo or in 4-player online co-op!

    Is it open world?
    Not quite. Cloudheim uses a hub-and-expedition structure. Each area is handcrafted, filled with secrets, enemies, and loot for you to discover.

    What can I do on the Odin Shell?
    It’s your home between adventures! Craft items, recruit blins, and tend to your store before diving back into battle.

    Are there classes or character customization?
    Yes, there are four class playstyles you can choose from: the Runeblade, Sentinel, Breaker, and Ranger. Each class has a unique set of weapon combos and skills to discover — you can also mix and match anything you’ve unlocked to discover a unique playstyle of your own.

    While there isn’t a character creator, you can choose to change your Runari appearance from several different options and skin variants, and freely at any time. It’s entirely cosmetic, so feel free to pick whoever you like the most!

    Demo & Early Access

    Is there a demo?
    Yes! The Cloudheim Demo is available now on Steam and Epic Games, featuring the full tutorial, Acts I & II of the story, and online co-op.

    How long is it?
    Most players spend 1.5 – 3 hours finishing the story content, with replay value from multiple classes and loot builds.

    Will my progress carry over?
    Not yet, as the demo is separate from the upcoming Early Access version.

    When is Early Access?
    Cloudheim is available now!

    Platforms & Technical

    What platforms will it be on?
    Cloudheim will be available for Windows PC (Steam & Epic) for Early Access. Console versions for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S are planned for 1.0.

    Is there cross-play?
    Steam and Epic players can play together. Broader cross-platform support is being explored.

    Can I play offline?
    At the moment, offline play is not officially supported.

    Is there controller support?
    Yes.

    What languages are supported?
    Cloudheim is available in English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese.

    Community

    How do I report bugs?
    Use Menu → Report a Bug or press F8 in-game. This automatically includes helpful info for the team and is the preferred way to report bugs!

    Where can I give feedback?
    Join the Cloudheim Discord! We review feedback daily during Early Access.

    Do you have a Creator Program?
    Yes, feel free to apply via Keymailer. Note: Spots are limited!

    Release & Future

    When will 1.0 release?
    We plan to have 1.0 release after the Early Access release, though the exact date is still to be determined. There will be a roadmap and updates shared along the way.

    Will it be free-to-play?
    No. Cloudheim is a premium game.

    Still have questions?
    Hop into the Cloudheim Discord and chat with the devs and community!
